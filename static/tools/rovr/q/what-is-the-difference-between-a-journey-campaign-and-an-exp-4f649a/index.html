what is the difference between a journey, campaign and an experiment in AJO?

Journey, Campaign, and Experiment in Adobe Journey Optimizer

Here are the key differences:

Journey vs. Campaign

Journeys are multi-step orchestrations that react to events or target audiences in real-time:

  • Provide 1:1 real-time orchestration where each profile progresses at its own pace through conditional logic
  • Triggered individually by events (e.g., purchase, app sign-in) or by audience qualification
  • Support complex logic, conditions, wait times, and multiple touchpoints across the customer lifecycle
  • Four types available: Unitary, Read Audience, Audience Qualification, and Business event journeys

Campaigns deliver messages simultaneously to an audience on a schedule or via API trigger:

  • Send targeted, one-time content to specific audiences across multiple channels
  • Execute coordinated marketing actions simultaneously
  • Deliver messages at a scheduled time or triggered via API
  • Can also be orchestrated campaigns for batch canvas workflows with complex multi-entity segmentation

Experiments

Experiments are randomized trials that test different content variations within both campaigns and journeys:

You can choose between three experiment types:

  • A/B experiment: You define the traffic split between treatments at the start. Performance is evaluated based on your chosen primary metric, and the Experimentation Accelerator reports the observed lift between treatments.
  • Multi-armed bandit: Traffic split between treatments is handled automatically. Every 7 days, performance on the primary metric is reviewed, and weights are adjusted accordingly.
  • Bring your own Multi-armed bandit: Traffic split is automatic with the flexibility to determine when and how it should change using the Experiment APIs to adjust allocations in real time.

Experiments measure outcome metrics you're interested in (opens, subscriptions, purchases, etc.) to identify which content variation performs best. They can be applied to actions within both campaigns and journeys to optimize content delivery.
