Finding Your Domain for Tag Properties in AEP

Based on the retrieved documentation, when creating a Tag Property in Adobe Experience Platform Data Collection, you need to enter your domain during the property setup.

What Domain to Enter

The domain you should enter is your website's domain — the actual domain where you plan to deploy the tag property and Web SDK.

Examples:

  • `example.com`
  • `www.example.com`
  • `mysite.co.uk`

Where to Get Your Domain

Your domain comes from:

1. Your company's web hosting provider — wherever your website is hosted

2. Your domain registrar — where you registered your domain name (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap, Domain.com, etc.)

3. Your IT/DevOps team — if your organization manages domains internally

How to Enter It in Tag Properties

When creating a new Tag Property in the Data Collection UI:

1. Navigate to Tags → Properties

2. Click New Property

3. Enter a Property Name (e.g., "My Website Tags")

4. Select Platform: Web

5. Enter your Domain (e.g., `example.com` or `www.example.com`)

6. Click Save

Important Notes

> The domain you enter helps Adobe organize your tag property and can affect cookie scope and other configurations related to your specific website.

> You can apply one tag property to multiple domains and subdomains if needed — for example, if you have multiple websites based on one template, you can apply a single property to all of them.

> The domain setting relates to how cookies from your tag property will be scoped. For example, if you configure a cookie with domain settings via the `_satellite.cookie.set()` method, the domain you specify in the property influences cookie visibility across your site structure.

If you don't yet have a domain, you'll need to:

1. Register a domain through a registrar

2. Point it to your hosting provider

3. Wait for DNS propagation (typically 24-48 hours)

4. Then use that domain when creating your Tag Property
