What are the cost differences between running Adobe Analytics versus Customer Journey Analytics?

I don't have information on the specific pricing or cost differences between Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics in the Adobe Experience League documentation available to me.

However, based on what I do have documented, I can tell you that:

  • Running both platforms simultaneously during a transition period is generally more expensive than running either one alone
  • Adobe does not recommend maintaining both Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics long-term due to cost and complexity considerations
  • Most organizations eventually disable Adobe Analytics after fully transitioning to CJA
